Timing Language & Safety Assessment

A data-driven framework designed to systematically evaluate language barriers, workplace safety risks, client-specific requirements, and key Arbo/ABU compliance controls to support safe and well-documented placement decisions for international flex workers.

About This Tool

The Language & Safety Matrix is an evidence-based assessment tool designed for Timing Emmen. It helps recruiters evaluate if a non-Dutch speaking candidate can safely perform a specific job role, based on task predictability, safety risks, and available mitigations.

How to use (3 steps):

  1. Fill in the Candidate Profile and the client's Job Requirements.
  2. Select available Mitigations & Readiness (e.g., Translation Apps, Buddies).
  3. Click Calculate Placement Score to generate a compliance check and actionable advice.

Disclaimer: This tool is an advisory instrument based on HR, Arbo and ABU-cao control points. It does not replace the client's RI&E, the written arbo information that must be provided before the assignment, payroll/cao checks, or legal review. Final responsibility remains with Timing/the legal employer and the client/inlener according to applicable law, cao and contract agreements.

Compliance scope: The tool supports checks for Arbo instruction, role-specific risk information, required qualifications, PPE/work equipment and ABU-cao information. It does not certify Wtta compliance; Wtta readiness must be tracked separately against the current implementation schedule.
